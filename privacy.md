
# Privacy Policy — Rclone Personal Archive

Last updated: November 12, 2026

## Purpose

Rclone Personal Archive is a private application used
to access the owner's Google Drive storage through Rclone.

It is not a publicly available service.

## Data Access

The application requests permission to access Google
Drive files for personal storage, backup, retrieval,
and encryption operations.

## Data Processing

Files are encrypted locally using Rclone Crypt before
being uploaded to the designated encrypted archive.

Google authentication credentials are stored locally
in the Rclone configuration.

## Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally share personal
file contents, Google authentication credentials,
or encryption keys with third parties.

Google Drive remains the cloud storage provider.

## Data Retention

Files remain in Google Drive until the account owner
deletes them, subject to Google's retention policies.

## Security

The application uses Google OAuth authorization and
Rclone's encryption capabilities.

## Contact

For questions about this personal-use application,
contact the application owner through the support
email provided in Google's OAuth consent configuration.
